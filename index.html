How to Pay via 17-digit PSID:

1. JazzCash / EasyPaisa:
Open App > Government Payments > Punjab > 1Bill / DLIMS > Enter 17-digit PSID voucher number > Pay.
2. Mobile Banking (HBL, Meezan, Bank of Punjab, UBL):
Bill Payment > Select 1Bill / PSID > Enter 17-digit PSID > Amount appears automatically > Confirm.
3. ATM or Commercial Bank:
Walk into any 1Link ATM or commercial bank branch in Punjab with the 17-digit PSID to deposit cash.
